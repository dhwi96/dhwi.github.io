I am a **PhD candidate** at the School of Computer Science and Engineering,
**Kyungpook National University**, advised by **Prof. Sang-hyo Park** in the
[Video Intelligence Lab.](https://sites.google.com/view/knuvi/about).

I received my M.S. degree from Kyungpook National University in February 2023,
also advised by Prof. Sang-hyo Park. My research spans deep-learning-based
computer vision, with a focus on visual restoration, generative models, and multi-view 3D vision.

## Research interests

- **Image processing:** video compression, super-resolution, denoising, and restoration
- **2D/3D vision:** novel-view synthesis, multi-view stereo, and spatial AI
- **Generative AI:** diffusion models and generative adversarial networks (GANs)
